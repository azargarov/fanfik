# Baseline translation prompt

Translate the supplied English fiction passage into natural Russian.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not invent or add events, actions, details, motivations, feelings, or explanations absent from the source.
2. Preserve facts, meaning, uncertainty, omissions, and subtext.
3. Preserve paragraphing, short sentences, repetition, pacing, and pauses where natural Russian permits. If a structural change is needed for natural Russian, make only the smallest necessary change.
4. Preserve the author's register, simplicity, irregularities, and intended artistic devices. Do not embellish plain prose.
5. Do not intensify, soften, or make implicit emotions explicit without evidence in the source.
6. Avoid calques, unnatural dialogue, clichés, and formulaic phrasing. Prefer natural Russian that carries the source meaning and voice.
7. Use minimal editorial freedom. Make only small, clearly justified improvements to obvious amateur-writing problems; when uncertain, preserve the source.

**Priority:** Literary polish or stylistic preference must never override meaning, source form, or authorial restraint.

Return only the Russian translation. Preserve the source paragraph breaks. Do not add commentary, a preface, notes, or explanations.
