# Current project state

Fanfik is a Codex-guided English-to-Russian book translation workflow. Its reusable components are documented in [README.md](README.md); operational steps are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), with stage prompts and an assessment rubric in their respective directories.

The prototype workflow and EPUB utilities are implemented. Focused tests use synthetic input. Translation quality and Dynamic Context's effect have not been established through independent bilingual evaluation or broad cross-book/model validation. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for limitations and [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md) for deferred questions.
